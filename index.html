Established 1932 • San Francisco, CA

A Unique Food Company with A Tradition of Quality

Since 1932, Liberty Gold Fruit Company LP has earned a worldwide reputation for excellence in service as well as in our premium quality food products. Still family-owned and operated, Liberty Gold is one of the few privately-owned companies left in the industry. The company has LIGO Brand products on grocery shelves in 40 countries worldwide, often as one of the leading brands, with extensive distribution in Asia, Central America, and Europe.
